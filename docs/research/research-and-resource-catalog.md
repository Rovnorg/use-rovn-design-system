# Rōvn professional document system: research and resource catalog

Current visual authority: [design direction, 3 October 2026](../design-direction.md). Historical amber/Crimson/Paper preferences below are dated research context and are superseded for new deliverables. Pure-white reading surfaces, the original Rōvn mark, expressive typography, deliberate amber and purpose-specific composition.

Research date: September 11, 2026. Status: completed initial research milestone and implementation recommendation. This document does not claim that proposed exports already work.

## Decision

Extend the maintained renderer into a document system with explicit intent, semantic content, distinct layout families and format-specific exporters. Preserve Jerry's assets and original report layout as a named option. Use conventional legal composition and the founder's light investor treatment as separate profiles.

The main problem is architectural. The current pipeline chooses `report-v1` before considering the reader's task. A library of additional decorative blocks would still leave memos, agreements and proposals inside that same report structure.

The companion [document taxonomy](document-taxonomy.json) defines 21 families, their document subtypes, reading jobs, structural requirements, useful formats and verification needs. This is a broad professional catalog, not a claim to cover every jurisdiction, profession or recipient-specific submission. Types can share a family while still having different authoring conventions. An executive memo and board resolution, for example, should not share the same required content.

Baseline verification on this Windows host: Node `v24.14.0`, npm `11.9.0`, all 12 repository tests passed. A short synthetic memo with four small sections rendered to six pages: cover, contents and four body pages. A visual inspection confirmed a large amber opener above a two-line paragraph. This demonstrates the selection problem without using private company content. Existing template correctness and document appropriateness require different tests.

Current capability is Markdown/DOCX/prepared JSON input to report PDF, standalone paginated HTML and QA artifacts. DOCX input does not mean DOCX output. PPTX, XLSX, fillable PDF, EPUB and recipient-specific filing exports were not implemented in the inspected code.

## Research method and selection criteria

Sources below are primary publishers, standards bodies, maintainers or original authors. Pages were accessed on September 11, 2026. Dates are the publication/version dates visible at inspection, or explicitly marked as undated/rolling. A search result's crawl date was not treated as the document's publication date.

Resources were evaluated for their actual use in this renderer: document conventions, semantic model, local execution, editability, pagination, tables, references, accessibility, licensing, dependency burden and testability. “Low/medium/high adaptation” is a relative engineering assessment, not a delivery estimate. No paid service was purchased or installed. Code licenses, template licenses, font licenses and image rights are separate.

An official template establishes its own context. It does not make that form universally suitable. Government procurement patterns, for example, can inform scope and acceptance structure without importing government clauses into a commercial SOW.

## What the research changes in the design

1. **Choose the reading job before layout.** A decision memo begins with a decision. A legal agreement begins with its operative structure. A workbook begins with assumptions and calculations. A live presentation must account for speaking and viewing distance.
2. **Make front matter optional.** A memo, letter, quote and brief case study generally need a compact opening. Long plans and technical reports may need contents. Decorative full-page covers become an intentional choice.
3. **Separate semantic structure from decoration.** Clauses, exhibits, signatures, milestones, typed money, source references and form fields are real objects. Cards and image bands are presentation choices.
4. **Use family-specific typography.** Preserve appropriate brand fonts while improving ordinary reading size. Legal documents receive conventional serif body text and restrained hierarchy. Numerical tables get explicit units and tabular alignment. Long URLs and identifiers wrap without changing their contents.
5. **Make editability concrete.** DOCX must have editable paragraphs and tables; PPTX must have editable native objects; XLSX must have typed cells and checked formulas. HTML source editing is a separate capability.
6. **Treat wide tables as a layout decision.** Use landscape schedules or a companion workbook, repeat headers and preserve all rows. Never reduce type until dense data technically fits but cannot be read.
7. **Preserve source content before and after rendering.** The existing pagination comparison is useful but cannot catch a prior authoring edit, altered legal numbering or a dropped importer feature. Add source-to-model and cross-export checks.
8. **Use real accessibility evidence.** Table/header relationships, heading navigation, reading order, contrast, alt text and field labels must be checked for each export. Requesting tagged PDF output does not prove conformance.

## Conflicts and research limitations

- Current product canon includes an operational style file with older teal/Source Serif/Hanken choices. The founder explicitly selected Jerry's installed amber/Crimson Pro/Inter/Fragment Mono system for this task. Preserve that selected system for these document adapters; do not silently rewrite product canon or live surfaces to resolve the difference.
- The fetched product revision was `1dd8ee5958f413db91bda9992fa771bd85fd41b0`; all 141 pinned files were validated after a successful fetch. The resolver was run with `--no-fetch` after that separate fetch, so its receipt labels itself offline. The fetch plus subsequent immutable validation is the evidence chain. Local `Rovn/current` had a different HEAD and was not used as current authority.
- Company memory had extensive existing modifications. Its charter and relevant design instruction were inspected; no company ledger or generated brain was changed. No company fact is needed in synthetic document fixtures.
- Some resource endpoints failed or redirected. The catalog uses successfully retrieved primary alternatives. Unverified licenses remain unverified. The initial study has not benchmarked every candidate renderer or certified accessibility.
- Public availability does not establish reuse permission for artwork, brand assets, legal text or downloaded templates. Preserve each resource's provenance and notices before bundling.
- The 21-family taxonomy is an extensible inventory. Specialist legal, clinical, regulatory, engineering-drawing, tax, shipping and archival workflows need their own exact schemas and recipients. A generic renderer must route these deliberately rather than announce support based on a filename.

## Delivery sequence

First implement the intent model and distinct narrative/legal families in the maintained repository. Then add the editable document, presentation and spreadsheet paths justified above, with explicit per-component support. Train the relevant shared skills to select these paths, preserve supplied material and verify results. Finally publish a local sample gallery and tested capability matrix. Any export that cannot be opened, inspected and validated stays marked experimental or unsupported.

The first representative set must include a one-page memo, legal agreement, business plan, technical report, proposal, formal letter, operating procedure, case study, pricing schedule, presentation and financial workbook. Long and difficult variants must exercise wide tables, notes, links, exhibits, long names, signatures and omitted optional sections. These are synthetic fixtures and do not establish legal review or company traction.
