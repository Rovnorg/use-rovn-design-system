# Approve the document production pilot

## Decision requested

Approve a two-week internal trial using synthetic documents. The trial will compare a concise memo, legal agreement, business plan and proposal, then record which structures help readers find the information they need.

## Why this matters

The baseline memo contains four brief sections but renders as six report pages. Its first content page puts a large image header above two lines of prose. A compact memo should put the decision and supporting evidence together.

## Proposed scope

- Retain every supplied sentence, number, qualification and reference.
- Produce editable files where the reader needs to revise or calculate.
- Inspect exports at normal reading size and record unsupported content.

## Next action

The example test owner will record the chosen family, exported formats and page count. The trial passes only when each output has intact content, readable typography and an appropriate structure. This fixture makes no claims about actual company operations.
