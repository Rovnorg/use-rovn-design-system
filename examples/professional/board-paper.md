# Decision requested

Approve a bounded internal pilot covering six synthetic document packages. Approval would authorize preparation and review only. It would not authorize public use, procurement, legal execution, or claims about production readiness.

# Recommendation and rationale

Use a family-specific layout for each package and retain the existing report format as an explicit option. The pilot will test whether that approach improves reading and editing without losing supplied content.

The alternative is to keep the current report-only workflow. That has lower implementation effort but leaves short correspondence and agreements without an appropriate structure.

# Options and trade-offs

| Option | Benefit | Cost or limitation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Retain report-only workflow | Familiar process | Poor fit for short or conventional documents |
| Pilot family-specific formats | Tests fit, editability and integrity | Requires new review fixtures |
| Replace the entire platform | Maximum architectural freedom | Broad migration outside this proposal |

# Resources and gates

The sponsor must confirm available reviewer time before the pilot begins. No budget or staffing commitment is assumed in this paper.

| Gate | Evidence required | Decision owner |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Preparation complete | Six sources, purpose and target formats | Coordinator |
| Quality review | Content checks and page-by-page findings | Reviewer |
| Pilot conclusion | Accepted files and explicit limitations | Sponsor |

# Principal risks

Editable package validation may pass while native Office layout differs. Font availability, application versions, and formula recalculation require separate checks. A polished legal sample may still be unsuitable for a real transaction; the pilot makes no legal-validity claim.
