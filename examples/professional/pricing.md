# Included and excluded

Each package includes a PDF and the supported editable source named in its agreed scope. Content research, legal review, financial assurance, translation, publication, and signature services are excluded.

Additional requests require a written scope and price before work begins. No recurring charge, renewal period, cancellation policy, or discount is assumed.

# Choosing a package

Choose by the intended reader and required content, not by page count alone. If the destination requires a prescribed template or unsupported format, confirm the adapter and review process before making a commitment.
